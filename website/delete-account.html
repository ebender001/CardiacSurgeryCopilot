Your data

Delete your Heart Team Prep account

Heart Team Prep is published by Bender Apps, LLC. You can delete your account and its associated data at any time, directly in the app or by emailing us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Heart Team Prep and sign in.
  2. Open the Account menu on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Can't access the app? Request deletion by email

Email support@benderapps.dev from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my Heart Team Prep account.” We will verify the request and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in credentials).
  • Every case you prepared, including reports and answers.
  • Usage and cost records tied to your account.
  • Your active sessions, so you are signed out on all devices.

What is not deleted

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store (Menu > Subscriptions > Heart Team Prep) or through your Apple ID account settings, before or after deleting. Purchase records held by Google or Apple are kept under their own policies.

See our Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
