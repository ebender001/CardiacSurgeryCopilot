How it works

From a real case to a conference-ready report, with the heart team's perspective built in.

  1. 1

    Describe the case

    Dictate or type a real case in your own words — no patient identifiers, just the clinical picture.

  2. 2

    Answer a few focused questions

    Heart Team Prep asks only what's still missing — nothing you've already told it.

  3. 3

    Review your report and the heart team's take

    A nine-section report with evidence/guidelines, plus three distinct specialty perspectives on the same case.

Built for real conference prep

Structured conference reports

Diagnosis through postoperative concerns, organized the way you'd actually present it — not a wall of text.

Honest about what's missing

A dedicated "missing information" section names the gaps and assumptions you'll want to confirm before you stand up.

Evidence and guidelines

Automatically search PubMed for abstracts relevant to your case's controversies and technical considerations.

Three heart-team perspectives

See how a surgeon, a non-interventional cardiologist, and an aggressive interventional cardiologist would each approach your case — and where they'd genuinely disagree.

Built specifically for cardiac surgery

Not a general surgery tool with a cardiac skin — dictation, terminology, and the whole workflow are built around cardiac surgery cases from the ground up.

  • Preoperative conference prep
  • Cardiac surgery vocabulary & dictation
  • Clinical reasoning practice
  • Evidence & guideline search
