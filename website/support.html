Getting started

What is Cardiac Surgery Copilot?

Cardiac Surgery Copilot is an educational tool for cardiac surgery trainees with two modes. In Preoperative Case Conference, you describe a case, answer a few focused follow-up questions, and the app produces a structured report — diagnosis, indication, missing information, operative strategy, alternatives, controversies, technical considerations, postoperative concerns, and evidence/guidelines. In What Would You Do, you describe a case and get a short presentation plus a starting question, then hold a live discussion with an AI attending that questions your reasoning.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You can create an account with Sign in with Apple or an email and password.

Which mode should I use?

Use Preoperative Case Conference when you're actually presenting a case at conference and want a structured, complete report. Use What Would You Do when you want to practice reasoning through a case out loud — it's built for rehearsal, not for producing a document.

Is Cardiac Surgery Copilot free?

Your first complete case preparation is free. After that, preparing additional cases requires an auto-renewing subscription purchased through your Apple ID account. See our Terms of Use for full subscription terms.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not through Cardiac Surgery Copilot directly. On your device, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, select Cardiac Surgery Copilot, and turn off auto-renew. Turning off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends prevents the next charge; it does not refund the current period.

Patient information and accuracy

Can I enter real patient information?

No. Do not enter real patient identifiers — names, medical record numbers, exact dates, contact information, or anything else that could identify a specific patient, staff member, or institution. Use de-identified or approximate details instead.

The app includes an on-device check that flags likely identifiers before they leave your device as a safety net, but it is not a substitute for your own judgment and cannot screen audio already sent for speech recognition. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is Cardiac Surgery Copilot HIPAA compliant?

Cardiac Surgery Copilot is an educational preparation tool. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment, institutional policy, or supervision. It is not intended to be used to store or transmit protected health information, and is not represented as a HIPAA "covered entity" or "business associate."

Is the AI-generated content accurate?

Conference reports, discussion replies, and the literature search are generated with the help of AI and may be incomplete, inaccurate, out of date, or occasionally fabricated. Always independently review and verify content — including citations — before relying on it or presenting it.

Dictation

How does dictation work?

Tap the microphone to dictate your case instead of typing. Speech recognition may run on your device or, when a network connection is available, on Apple's speech recognition servers (this allows the app to better recognize cardiac surgery terminology). Only the recognized text — never the audio itself — is sent to the Cardiac Surgery Copilot backend, and it passes through the on-device patient-information check first.

Dictation isn't working — what should I check?

Make sure Cardiac Surgery Copilot has microphone and speech recognition permission in Settings, and that you have a network connection for the best recognition quality. If it still isn't working, you can always type the case instead — or contact us with details.

Your data

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at support@benderapps.dev from the address associated with your account and request deletion. See our Privacy Policy for more on how your data is handled.

Who can see the cases I create?

Your cases — from either mode — are associated with your account and are not visible to other users. See our Privacy Policy for details on how case content is stored and processed, including by our AI and literature-search providers.

Troubleshooting

Something isn't working — what should I do?

Email us with a short description of what happened, your iOS version, which mode you were using, and (if it's specific to one case) which case it involved. Screenshots help too.
